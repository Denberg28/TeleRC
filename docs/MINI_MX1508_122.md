# Mini rover MX1508 1.2.2 — drive and steering response

Keep the full-interface TeleRC APK **0.8.56**. This firmware-only candidate uses the same Wi-Fi protocol, arming rules, diagnostics and GPIO mapping as 1.2.1. No APK reinstall or rewiring is needed.

The user reports that 1.2.1 now moves, but steering beeps and drive is very slow. This establishes user-observed movement; motor speed, sound source, current, supply and stopping have not been measured. The old settings were a 1 kHz audible PWM carrier, a 180/255 drive ceiling, and steering gain 0.5 (at most 90/255 for a centered-drive pivot at 100% app sensitivity). Those settings can contribute to the reported symptoms; they do not establish the sole physical cause.

## Changes

For **MX1508 only**, straight-drive maximum and per-motor caps rise from 180 to **255/255**, and steering gain rises from 0.5 to **1.0**, so full pivot can use full duty. Combined drive/steering still normalizes the two sides within ±255. Neutral is exactly zero; response stays proportional with no minimum-duty jump or automatic startup pulse. Reversal still coasts for 30 ms. Other driver profiles retain their 180 caps and 0.5 steering gain.

MX1508 PWM returns to **20 kHz** to reduce the prior 1 kHz motor tone. This is an engineering test setting, not a guarantee of silence or starting torque on the installed motor/driver. At maximum duty the active input is continuously on. Other profile frequencies are unchanged. If only steering remains weak, inspect wheel scrub/mechanical load and driver supply under load; do not diagnose the driver solely from sound.

This enables full motor-supply duty; it does not raise supply voltage or regulate motor current. The default profile declares **at most 5 V motor supply and 6 V-rated motors**. Verify actual ratings and supply; supply must not exceed motor rating. Both MX1508 power inputs need motor power and common GND with the ESP32. Do not power motors from ESP32 3.3 V.

## Upload and test

1. Remove motor power for upload. Open `TeleRC_MiniRover_WiFi/TeleRC_MiniRover_WiFi.ino` with all headers kept together. Arduino ESP32 **3.3.2**, **ESP32S3 Dev Module**, **USB CDC On Boot Enabled**.
2. Raise the wheels and keep the motor-power cutoff accessible. Open Serial Monitor at **115200 baud**; confirm `v=1.2.2-mx1508-drive drv=MX1508 hz=20000`.
3. In TeleRC Setup set sensitivity to **100% for the raised-wheel test**. Select Mini rover Wi-Fi, join TeleRC-MiniRover / telerc123, Connect → Controls → Enable → centered ARM ready → ARM. Wait for heartbeat-confirmed ARMED / DISARM button. Start with a small command; full duty is higher than 1.2.1.
4. With steer centered, full forward should show `CH1=1500 CH2=1900 OUT=255/255/255/255` and `PWM=255,0/255,0/255,0/255,0`. Full reverse is the opposite input leg after reversal coast. Full right pivot, drive centered, should show `OUT=255/255/-255/-255`; full left is opposite. If physical direction differs, correct only that motor's `MOTOR_INVERT` after testing each wheel.
5. Release sticks: output zero. Verify DISARM, STOP, local BOOT stop and Wi-Fi command loss all remove output. Reconnection must require centered neutral and explicit ARM again. Measure electrical zero separately from mechanical stopping. Stop testing if a driver overheats or a wheel stalls under power.
6. Only after restrained-wheel checks, try brief ground motion at reduced input with cutoff ready. Record whether steering noise and low speed persist under load.

**Sensitivity remains effective.** At 25% sensitivity, full straight stick gives only `CH2=1600` and about **51/255 duty**; at 100% it reaches 1900 and 255. Firmware cannot turn a deliberately reduced command into full power without defeating the sensitivity control. For a small command, a stationary motor may hum below its starting torque. We do not force a minimum output.

If still slow, capture **one line at full forward and one at full steering**, with wheels raised and sensitivity 100%. If CH2 stops below 1900, investigate app input/sensitivity. If OUT and PWM reach 255 but speed remains low, measure motor-supply voltage at each driver while commanded, motor voltage, friction and driver temperature. PWM readback is a peripheral register, not proof of motor voltage or speed. If `ready=0 err>0 reason=PWM_ERROR`, output is latched off; save the line, remove motor power, then reboot.

## Verification and rollback

Regression checks reproduce the old full-drive limit, then verify exact forward/pivot pins and full duty, proportional intermediate demand, combined-input normalization, neutral, reversal coast, command loss, release/disarm, corrupt packets, blocked serial and injected PWM failure. The 20 driver/count/API host matrix verifies other profiles retain their limits. Required actual target builds use core 3.3.2 for six configurations. No app files changed; Android 0.8.56 retains run 38696307553 evidence.

Hardware sound/torque, current/temperature and stop timing remain **NOT TESTED by the agent**. Keep 1.2.1 for rollback: remove motor power, upload its original sketch and headers, then repeat neutral/arm/stop checks. Do not change APK signing identity or erase app data/NVS for this firmware-only change. This is a prerelease candidate; stable readiness needs measured hardware checks.

Reference: [Espressif LEDC API](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/arduino-esp32/en/latest/api/ledc.html). It documents LEDC frequency/resolution and duty APIs; it does not validate the installed motor or MX1508 module.
