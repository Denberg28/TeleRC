## Changes and review

Switching Light/Sepia/Dark used Activity.recreate(), which closed the link, cleared the cached wallpaper and exposed the launch/flat background before asynchronous decoding completed. Theme selection now resets the native theme attributes and replaces the view hierarchy synchronously within the same Activity. The already decoded wallpaper and transport session remain owned by that Activity. Endpoint drafts and update status survive the replacement. Repeated selection of the active theme/accent does nothing. Background selection closes without a platform window animation; no delayed crossfade or stacked render tasks are introduced. The window fallback is set to the selected background rather than the fixed launch color. Initial process startup still loads a bounded image asynchronously; this release removes the repeated theme-change reload.

Tab dropdown rows are now 40 dp instead of 48 dp at normal font size, scale with system text, retain the exact anchor width/start edge and 4 dp gap, and scroll when necessary. Header/footer bars retain their original sizes. All three tabs share the same menu. Existing centered, unanimated practice popups, crisp caption strips, transparent outer joystick panels, wallpaper opacity, Setup minimize/restore, fixed levels, three-bump restart/red dots, Market and bounded coin economy are retained.

No motor, protocol, firmware, permission, image format or wallet schema changes. No paired upgrade or firmware reflash is needed. Appearance never enables or arms control. Test still disconnects and disables auto-connect; actual driving requires explicit connection and authority steps.

## Install, next physical checks and rollback

Install **TeleRC-v0.8.75.apk** over the existing same-signer app without uninstalling. Preserve settings, private wallpaper, routes, level progress and wallet. In Setup, enter an unsaved endpoint draft and cycle Light → Sepia → Dark → Light repeatedly, then all accents. Confirm the wallpaper does not flash or reload, fields remain intact, native dialogs match the active palette and taps do not cause a reconnect. Repeat without a photo and with larger system text. On every tab check compact menu alignment, selection, outside/back dismissal and safe insets. Check Controls/Test label clarity and wallpaper opacity at 0%, 70% and 100%; Setup stays clear. Open Levels/restart/Market and verify stable centered placement. Check minimize/restore and update actions.

For restrained-wheel acceptance: isolate drive power first, connect the real receiver/rover, and confirm retained setup plus manual Connect/Enable/ARM behavior. With wheels raised, an accessible power cutoff and no people in the wheel plane, verify neutral and DISARM, loss-of-command stopping, no automatic enable/arm after reconnection or a theme/tab change, and Test disconnect/offline isolation. Stop on any unexpected output. Check real USB attach/default-app selection, FPV format, preview and pause/resume separately. Record phone/receiver/firmware identity and measured results before making physical reliability claims.

Retain v0.8.74 source/APK and v0.8.64 stable assets. Prefer a higher-code same-signer forward revert to preserve app data; Android normally rejects a version-code downgrade and uninstall loses private data.
